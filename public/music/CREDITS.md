Leading silence is trimmed, constant gain makes the excerpts audible on phones, and short fades frame each excerpt. There is no time stretching, quantisation of the performance, pitch shifting, synthetic accompaniment, or dynamic compression. Performance cues are sampled from audible attacks in the recording, rather than forcing the pianist to a fixed-tempo grid.

See `rights.json` for each exact source page/download URL, original and excerpt SHA-256 hashes, source offset, constant gain, encoding details and verification date. MP3 encoding is lossy. The 48-second versions are game edits, not the complete works. Attribution does not imply endorsement by the pianist.

The other 25 tracks use the existing electronic synthesiser/compositions inherited from the separate `claude/neon-overhaul` setup. They are labelled and grouped separately from Piano Sessions.
